How to play

Gather gold and lumber, build up your settlement, raise an army and destroy every enemy unit and building.

Left-click / dragSelect units (Shift adds)
Double-clickSelect all of that type on screen
Right-clickMove, attack, mine gold, chop trees, set rally point
A, then clickAttack-move: fight everything on the way
BBuild menu (with workers selected)
E, then clickRepair a damaged building (or right-click it with workers)
E / U / TSpellcasters: heal, summon, storm
Menu → Save gameSave; the game also autosaves every minute
Ctrl+1–9 / 1–9Assign / recall control groups (tap twice to jump)
. (period)Cycle idle workers
SpaceJump to last attack or selection
Arrows / screen edge / middle-dragScroll the map
+ / − or pinchZoom in and out
EscCancel
P or F10Pause

On a phone or tablet

TapSelect a unit, or command the selected units (move, attack, mine, chop)
Double-tap a unitSelect all of that type on screen
DragScroll the map · Pinch to zoom
Hold, then dragDraw a selection box (hold and release to deselect)
Tap a buttonShows what it does and uses it

There are four gold mines: one at each base and two out in the wilds, marked in gold on the minimap.

Tip: every unit needs food — build farms. Ranged units need a lumber workshop, riders a stable, siege a forge and spellcasters a temple.

Norsecraft

A real-time strategy saga of the North

Foe
Speed

Best played with a mouse and keyboard on a desktop browser.

How to play

Gather gold and lumber, build up your settlement, raise an army and destroy every enemy unit and building.

Left-click / dragSelect units (Shift adds)
Double-clickSelect all of that type on screen
Right-clickMove, attack, mine gold, chop trees, set rally point
A, then clickAttack-move: fight everything on the way
BBuild menu (with workers selected)
E, then clickRepair a damaged building (or right-click it with workers)
Ctrl+1–9 / 1–9Assign / recall control groups (tap twice to jump)
. (period)Cycle idle workers
SpaceJump to last attack or selection
Arrows / screen edge / middle-dragScroll the map
EscCancel
P or F10Pause

Tip: every unit needs food — build farms. Ranged units need a lumber workshop, riders a stable, siege a forge.
